Digital Ethics: Be a Light Online

Think Before You Click

Everything we post online can affect other people. Before sharing a comment, photo, or information, we should think about its possible consequences. Responsible digital citizens use technology carefully and respectfully.

Practice Kindness Online

Words can hurt even when they are written on a screen. We should avoid cyberbullying, respect different opinions, and communicate politely. Instead of spreading hate, we can use social media to encourage and support others.

Be Honest in Digital Creation

Honesty is important when creating schoolwork and digital content. We should do our own work, give credit to the original creators, and avoid copying someone else's ideas without permission or proper acknowledgment.

Protect Privacy and Share Wisely

  • Keep passwords and personal information private.
  • Ask permission before posting another person's photo.
  • Check if information is trustworthy before sharing it.
  • Respect other people's work and digital rights.

Let Your Light Shine Online

Technology is a powerful tool that can spread knowledge, faith, and kindness. By being honest, respectful, and responsible online, we can make the digital world a better place and become a light to others.

"Use technology to inspire, not to harm."
