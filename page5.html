Spreading God's Light Online

Technology as a Blessing

Technology is not only for entertainment and schoolwork. It can also be used to share inspiring messages, learn about faith, and encourage people who are having a hard time. Through responsible use of the internet, we can spread kindness and remind others that they are not alone.

Ways to Share Faith Through Technology

  • Share Bible verses and inspirational messages online.
  • Post encouraging words instead of hurtful comments.
  • Watch or share worship songs and faith-based videos.
  • Join online prayer groups or church activities.
  • Use digital skills to create meaningful and helpful content.

Be a Light on Social Media

Before posting anything, we should ask ourselves if our words can help, encourage, or inspire someone. We must also respect different people, protect their privacy, and make sure the information we share is trustworthy. Our online actions should reflect the values we believe in.

My Inspiration

"Let your light shine before others."

Matthew 5:16

This verse reminds me to use my talents, actions, and even my online presence to do good and inspire others.

My Digital Faith Commitment

As a student, I will do my best to use technology wisely. I will choose honesty over cheating, kindness over cyberbullying, and helpful content over negativity. I believe that even small positive actions online can make a difference in someone's life.

Let us use technology not only to connect, but also to uplift.
