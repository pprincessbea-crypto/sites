My Faith, My Values, My Digital World

Putting God First

As a student, I believe that putting God first means allowing my faith to guide my decisions. Whenever I face challenges, I try to remember the importance of patience, kindness, and doing what is right. For me, faith is not only about what I believe, but also about how I treat other people.

Choosing Honesty Every Day

I believe that honesty matters even when nobody is watching. As a student, I should do my own work, respect other people's ideas, and admit my mistakes. Being honest may not always be easy, but it helps me become a better person.

Think Before You Click

Technology helps us learn, communicate, and express ourselves. However, we should also be careful about what we post and share. I want to use technology to spread useful information, respect others online, and avoid sharing things that may hurt or mislead people.

!REMINDER!

Let Your Light Shine. Even through small actions, we can show our faith, choose honesty, and use technology to make a positive difference in the lives of others.
